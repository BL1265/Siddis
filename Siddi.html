01WHO THEY ARE

Who Are the Siddis?

The Siddis are communities of African origin living in India. They have been part of Indian society for centuries and developed a unique African-Indian cultural identity.

Who they are todayWhere they liveRough population
02 · Origins

The African Origins of the Siddis

Many Siddis trace their ancestry to sub-Saharan Africa, especially Bantu-speaking populations. Historical and genetic research supports their African ancestry.

East African coastBantu peoplesMeaning of "Siddi"
03 · The crossing

The Indian Ocean: A Bridge Between Africa and India

The Indian Ocean connected East Africa with India through trade and travel. These routes helped people move between Africa and the Indian subcontinent.

Monsoon windsTrade routesPorts & dhows
04 · Arrival

How Did Africans Reach India?

Africans reached India in different circumstances — as merchants, sailors, soldiers, and, in many cases, through the Indian Ocean slave trade.

By tradeMerchants and sailors working the Indian Ocean routes for centuries.
By serviceSoldiers and mercenaries hired by Indian rulers for their skill and loyalty.
By forceMany were brought against their will through the Indian Ocean slave trade.
05 · Strength

Siddis as Soldiers and Warriors

Siddis served in the armies and courts of several Indian states. Some rose to important military and political positions.

Naval powerJanjira fortLoyalty & discipline

African-origin leaders and soldiers played important roles in places such as the Deccan and Janjira. Their history became part of India's diverse political and cultural story.

08 · Fusion

Siddi Culture: Africa Meets India

Siddi culture is a unique blend of East African heritage and South Asian traditions. The Siddi people integrate local regional customs, languages and religions while preserving distinct ancestral music, dance and community ritual.

09 · The heartbeat

Music, Dance, and Traditions

DhamalA vibrant devotional dance and musical expression featuring rhythmic drumming and call-and-response chanting.
InstrumentsTraditional percussive instruments like the dammam (large bass drum), the duf and the gumte.
Cultural roleA vital link to African roots and communal identity during festivals and celebrations.
Dhamal danceGoma drumsSongs of ancestors
10 · Faith & home

Religion and Community Life

Religious diversitySiddis practise Islam (mostly Sufi), Hinduism and Christianity depending on the region, yet share underlying cultural customs.
SyncretismMany groups blend regional folk deities with ancestor worship, such as honouring ancestral spirits known as hiriyaru or hireru.
IntegrationThey speak local languages like Gujarati, Kannada, Konkani or Urdu, wearing regional attire while maintaining a tight-knit community structure.
11 · Today
“
African by ancestry. Indian by home. Siddi by identity.

Granted Scheduled Tribe (ST) status in several states, their modern identity focuses on overcoming socioeconomic marginalisation and colorism, and on seeking political representation.

Scheduled Tribe statusSport & educationDual heritage
12 · The hard part

Challenges Faced by the Siddi Community

The Siddi community faces persistent racial, social and economic marginalisation.

PovertyWidespread poverty across Siddi communities.
Education & healthLow literacy rates and limited access to quality healthcare and education.
DiscriminationAcute racial and social discrimination due to their distinct African ancestry.
13 · Keeping it alive

Preserving Siddi Heritage

Siddi heritage — an Afro-Indian diaspora rooted in centuries of migration, resilience and cross-cultural blending — is kept alive through oral histories, rhythmic Dhamaal beats, sacred shrine traditions and community memory.

Passing on musicOral storiesCultural festivals
14LEGACY

The Siddi Contribution to Indian History

The Siddi community, descendants of East African Bantu peoples arriving via trade and servitude since the 7th century, shaped Indian history through military leadership, naval defence and unique cultural blending in the Deccan and western coastal regions.

Two Roots, One Home

🌍 African Roots

Ancestry, rhythm and memory — carried across the Indian Ocean and never let go of.

🇮🇳 Indian Home

Centuries of soldiering, building, worship and belonging in Gujarat, Karnataka, Maharashtra and Goa.

African roots + Indian home = the Siddi story

The story of the Siddis shows the long historical connection between Africa and India, and highlights migration, cultural exchange, leadership and resilience.

The Siddi community continues to face severe economic marginalisation and social isolation. Expanding structural support and safeguarding their Scheduled Tribe status are essential steps to secure their education, land rights and equitable future in India.
